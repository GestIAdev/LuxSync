fix(bpm): CLEAN THE CHASSIS & FIX THE LOGGER

Two structural fixes to the BPM downstream chassis. TempoOracle untouched.

1. Fix false-alarm logger (TickEngine.ts):
   The [TitanOrchestrator] BPM log was printing pre-shield workerBpm
   (before hysteresis gate + EMA), causing false alarms when the Oracle
   temporarily jumped to octave-down (e.g., 124 to 62) but the gate
   correctly rejected it. Moved the logger to AFTER both shields so it
   reports the final context.bpm actually sent to TitanEngine/VMM.
   Format changed from "WORKER BPM=X (raw=Y)" to "BPM=X (raw=Y)" to
   reflect post-shield value.

2. Remove double EMA smoothing (VibeMovementManager.ts):
   The TickEngine already smooths BPM via EMA (alpha=0.15, ~4s converge).
   The VMM applied a second, slower EMA (alpha=0.05, ~20f converge) on
   top, causing lights to lag ~2 seconds behind the orchestrator.
   Removed the VMM EMA entirely. VMM now just clamps the incoming
   (already-smoothed) audio.bpm via getSafeBPM() [60,200] and uses it
   directly. Renamed smoothedBPM to activeBpm to reflect no smoothing
   happens here anymore. Updated SEALED_PARAMS.ts audit trail.
