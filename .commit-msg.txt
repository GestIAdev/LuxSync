feat(bpm): add shield status tag to BPM logger

The post-shield BPM logger now shows a visual tag when the hysteresis
gate acts on a large BPM jump, so you can see at a glance whether the
shield is working:

  BPM=124.07 (raw=62.53) conf=0.14 ⚠️OCT-DOWN🛡️ | PLL=LOCKED ...
  BPM=124.07 (raw=124.07) conf=0.72                | PLL=LOCKED ...

Tags:
  (empty)        = small/normal change, accepted
  🛡️ACCEPT       = large change confirmed after 60f, accepted
  🛡️HOLD         = large change pending confirmation, holding stable
  🛡️REJECT       = large change, new candidate (reset counter)
  ⚠️OCT-DOWN🛡️   = octave-down (÷2) rejected/holding
  ⚠️OCT-DOWN✅    = octave-down confirmed after 60f @ conf>0.7 (rare)
  ⚠️OCT-UP🛡️     = octave-up (×2) rejected/holding
  ⚠️OCT-UP✅      = octave-up confirmed after 60f @ conf>0.7 (rare)

Octave detection uses ratio thresholds 0.45-0.55 (÷2) and 1.85-2.15 (×2).
