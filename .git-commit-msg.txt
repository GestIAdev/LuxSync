fix(selene): WAVE 7559 — YIN YANG 2.0: Markov posterior thresholding + heuristic reorder

Field log analysis (buscandoambient2.md, 602 lines) revealed that
breakdown_imminent and energy_drop NEVER fired, so the YIN YANG zone
override (peak/intense to gentle/ambient) never triggered proactively.
Two root causes:

Fix 1 — Markov Posterior Thresholding:
- breakdown is never the argmax of the Markov posterior. In fiesta-latina,
  chorus to buildup wins 67% vs chorus to breakdown 33%. The argmax is
  ALWAYS buildup or chorus, so PTYPE[argmax] was never breakdown_imminent.
- The WAVE 7558 structural bonus (+0.15) was structurally broken: it
  assumed breakdown_imminent lost the predictCombined arbitration, but
  in reality predict() never returned breakdown_imminent at all.
- Fix: expose pBreakdownMass (POST[6] after normalization). If
  P(breakdown) > 0.25 (minority significance threshold), force
  predictionType = breakdown_imminent even when breakdown is not the
  argmax. The probability is calculated from the breakdown mass (not
  the argmax mass), reflecting real confidence. The WAVE 7558 bonus
  then boosts it further in predictCombined arbitration.
- The 0.25 threshold is conservative: breakdowns are more common than
  pure spectral buildups (which need spectralScore > 0.4), but
  0.25 is enough to surface breakdown_imminent without false-positiving
  on noise.

Fix 2 — Heuristic Evaluation Order (predictFromEnergy):
- DROP DETECTION (tension > 0.4 + trend falling) was evaluated BEFORE
  FALLING ENERGY (trend falling + E < 0.5). In Latin music (constant
  high tension), DROP DETECTION hijacked every falling-energy state,
  returning drop_incoming even when energy was at 0.30.
- Fix: moved FALLING ENERGY check ABOVE DROP DETECTION. Additionally
  constrained DROP DETECTION to require currentEnergy > 0.5 — a real
  drop implies falling FROM A HIGH POINT. If energy is already below
  0.5, a falling trend is an energy_drop (breakdown), regardless of
  tension. This restores the energy_drop heuristic path that was
  completely unreachable in high-tension vibes.
