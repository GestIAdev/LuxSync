Eradicates the constant-tempo assumption from the offline pipeline. The

1. TEMPO CURVE PERSISTENCE (analysisPipeline.ts)
The TempoOracle already produced a per-frame BPM estimate; it was being
discarded after the confidence-weighted median reduced it to a scalar.
Note that the existing `bpmSamples` array could NOT be reused: it is
packed (confident frames only, contiguous), so index k has no relation
to frame k. Added `tempoRaw`, a sparse-by-index companion that preserves
the time axis, then `buildTempoCurve()`:
  - Stage 1 gap fill: zero-order hold across sub-CONF_FLOOR frames.
    Interpolating would invent a tempo ramp the music does not contain;
    absent evidence, the correct prior is "tempo unchanged".
  - Stage 2 sliding median (21 frames ≈ 1.05 s). A mean would smear a
    single octave flip by +6 BPM across the window; the median is immune.
Persisted as LuxAnalysisV3.tempoCurve so Hephaestus can phase-lock curve
durations to LOCAL tempo instead of a global average.

2. ELLIS DP BEAT TRACKER (trackBeatsDP)
Replaces `for (t = first; t < end; t += msPerBeat)` with the Ellis 2007
dynamic program maximising  Σ O(bᵢ) + α·Σ −log²((bᵢ−bᵢ₋₁)/P).
Deviation from the paper: P is indexed per frame from the tempo curve
rather than being a single global estimate. That is the substantive
difference between "constant-tempo grid" and "tempo map" — a ritardando
or DJ pitch-ride moves P with it, so the transition cost stops fighting
the music.
Also added `buildOnsetEnvelope`: heatmap.flux is FULL-wave rectified, so
an energy collapse reads identically to an attack and would plant a
phantom beat on every release. The new envelope is half-wave rectified
and kick-weighted, normalised to unit variance so Ellis's published
tightness=100 transfers without retuning.
Sub-frame parabolic refinement recovers the ±25 ms frame quantisation.
Falls back to the legacy uniform grid when the DP cannot lock (ambient
material), surfaced via BeatGridData.variableTempo.

3. SPECTRAL DOWNBEAT & METRE (detectDownbeats)
Replaces `(beats.length − 1) % 4 === 0`, which detected nothing — it
counted, and declared beat 1 to be wherever the tracker happened to
start. Now scores metrical CONTRAST per hypothesis:
    S(m,p) = (kick[down] − kick[other]) + (snare[back] − snare[down])
Contrast rather than raw energy is essential: four-on-the-floor has high
kick energy at every phase, so only the difference between hypothesised
strong and weak positions carries the metre. Metre detection (3 vs 4) is
included but deliberately biased toward 4/4 — a false waltz corrupts the
whole grid, so 3/4 must win by 15%.
Documented a genuine limit: a perfectly symmetric kick|snare|kick|snare
bar makes phase p and p+2 score identically. That information is not
present in the percussion; it is an idiom property, not an estimator bug.
