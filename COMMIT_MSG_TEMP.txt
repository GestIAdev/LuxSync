WAVE 7749.52: Morning Exorcism — Selective TCT + Onset-Gated Zones

Two surgical fixes to the d9d1e507 Golden State base, addressing the
metric divergence diagnosed in WAVE 7749.48.

FIX 1: Selective TCT for Back R (Morphological Lock)
  Problem: The High-Flux Bypass (spectralFlux > 0.20) and Energy Bypass
  (snareEnergy > 0.40) fired on ANY frame with high spectralFlux, including
  sustained synth sweeps in reggaeton/melodic techno. A synth sweep with
  filter open has Flux 0.20-0.35 frame after frame → continuous false
  onsets → hallucinated snare rolls.

  Fix: Added _prevRawSnareDelta tracking + _snareReArmed flag. The detector
  re-arms only when the previous frame's rawSnareDelta has settled below
  0.02 (near-zero), proving a sharp transient edge. Paths 2 (High-Flux)
  and 3 (Energy) now require _snareReArmed to fire. Path 1 (WNS) is EXEMPT
  — WNS already proves broadband noise, which no synth sweep produces.

  Result: Sustained synth sweeps can no longer hold the snare onset open.
  Real snare transients (which spike and decay in 1-2 frames) re-arm the
  detector naturally and fire without restriction.

FIX 2: Onset-Gated Floor & Air Zones
  Problem: The Golden State floorIntensity was a raw passthrough:
    (subBass × 0.65 + lowMid × 0.35) × recoveryFactor
  This reacted to sustained amplitude, not transients. A reggaeton bassline
  with wobble/LFO caused floorIntensity to oscillate → Front Par strobed.
  Similarly, _airEMA was a soft follower with ~8 frame attack delay.

  Fix: Restored envFloor and envAir (LiquidEnvelope instances) with
  fallback defaults. Floor is now fed by bassDelta × 2.0 (transient impact
  only) — a sustained bassline has bassDelta ≈ 0 after the first frame,
  so envFloor decays to 0. Air is fed by (treble × 0.6 + highMid × 0.4)
  through envAir with zero-attack, fast decay (0.08), high gate (0.35).

  Added envelopeFloor? and envelopeAir? to ILiquidProfile for genre-specific
  overrides. Profiles without these fields use the DEFAULT_ENVELOPE_FLOOR
  and DEFAULT_ENVELOPE_AIR constants.

  Result: Floor reacts to kick/note onsets, not sustained bass. Air reacts
  to treble stabs in 1 frame instead of 8. No more strobing on sustained
  sub-bass or lagging aerial laser responses.
