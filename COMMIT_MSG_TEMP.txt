The ghost path (trebleGhost) was designed for dead-gate tracks (Opus: gH=0.0)
where the crack gate can't see snares. But it doesn't check gateHealth, so in
alive-gate tracks (Minimal: gH=1.0) it fires on every hhDlt spike.

The 20 ghost-driven onsets are multi-triggers: moderate SnareE (0.4-0.6),
high hhDlt (0.24-0.50), but crack path can't see them (Res/cFx too low).
These are synth stabs or hi-hat fluctuations, not real snares.

Fix: scale ghost by (1-gateHealth):
  ghostGateFactor = 1.0 - gateHealth
  trebleGhost = ... * ghostGateFactor

without ghost. The instantGateDead fix (WAVE 7749.96) handles dead-gate
misses via boosted sEF/crackDrive, not via ghost.
