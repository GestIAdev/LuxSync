WAVE 7749.48: Remove L0 LiquidEngine strobe injection — effects own strobe

The LiquidAetherAdapter was injecting strobe intents (strobeRate=1.0,
shutter=1.0) to all IMPACT nodes whenever calculateStrobe() detected a
treble peak. This L0 strobe competed with L3 (.lfx) strobe curves,
causing interference when both were active — especially during effect
overlaps (e.g. Solar Flare + Seismic Snap).

Root cause: the LiquidEngineBase.calculateStrobe() is a legacy reactive
strobe detector from before Hephaestus effects existed. Now that every
strobe effect has its own .lfx curve with precise strobe timing, the
L0 reactive strobe is redundant and harmful.

Fix: removed the _routeStrobeNodes() call from LiquidAetherAdapter.ingest().
The strobe scratch objects remain as dead code (commented out) for
potential future reactivation per-vibe. The calculateStrobe() in
LiquidEngineBase still runs (harmless — its result is just ignored by
the adapter).

Tests updated:
- §2.1-§2.3: now verify L0 NEVER emits shutter/strobe/strobeRate
- §4.1: now verifies L0 never emits shutter regardless of strobeActive
- selene-aether-adapter Test 4: verifies physicsModifier never emits
  strobe (WAVE 7749.46 neutralization)

Combined with WAVE 7749.46 (physicsModifier neutralized) and 7749.47
(strobeRate in STRICT_PRIORITY), the strobe channel now has exactly
ONE source: the .lfx curve. If the clip says strobe=1, it fires at 1.
