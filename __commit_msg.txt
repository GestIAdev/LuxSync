WAVE 7626: The Final Polish — DMX clamp + EMA smoothing + airbag reset

Three fixes addressing the clipping and airbag panic issues identified
by forensic report WAVE 7625:

1. DMX DELTA CLAMP (NodeResolver.ts)
   - Added MAX_OFFSET_DMX = 100 (±100 DMX, ~40% of 255 range) and
     MAX_OFFSET_DMX_16 = 25700 for 16-bit path.
   - In _writeNodeIK, after computing panDelta/tiltDelta, clamp to
     ±MAX_OFFSET_DMX before adding to logicalPan/logicalTilt.
   - This prevents distScale amplification (up to 2.0x) from pushing
     the L2 pattern offsets into the 0/255 boundary clamp, which
     caused the "20% clip" where fixtures stuck at the sweep range
     end for a couple of seconds before joining the return path.

2. EMA OFFSET SMOOTHING (AetherKineticEngine.ts)
   - Replaced the WAVE 7624 _offsetFadeIn (reset to 0 + linear ramp)
     with an Exponential Moving Average (_smoothOffsetX/_smoothOffsetY).
   - EMA_ALPHA = 0.05 → time constant ~20 frames ≈ 0.45s at 44Hz.
   - The EMA smoothly interpolates from the previous offset value to
     the new pattern's target, acting as a low-pass filter. Pattern
     switches, amplitude changes, and phase resets all produce a
     smooth transition with no single-frame spike.
   - The fade-in's step function (reset to 0) triggered the
     AetherSafetyMiddleware velocity clamp, causing the "airbag panic"
     where fixtures pointed randomly at the ceiling/sides.

3. AIRBAG STATE RESET (AetherSafetyMiddleware.ts + AetherIPCHandlers.ts)
   - Added resetKineticState(nodeId) method to AetherSafetyMiddleware
     that sets KS_INIT = 0, letting the fixture snap to a new position
     without the velocity clamp fighting the transition.
   - Added getSafetyMiddleware() getter to NodeResolver.
   - In setManualPattern IPC handler, after setManualKinetics, calls
     resetKineticState for all affected nodes. This prevents the
     clamp's KS_LAST_PAN/TILT from being stuck at the old offset-
     polluted position and slowly tracking back.
