WAVE 7618.2: Fix amplitude — correct pan/tilt scaling + map tilt to Y axis

The target mutation was producing tiny angular changes ("amplitud de
chiste") even at 100% amplitude. Three issues fixed:

1. PAN SCALING: The L2 engine scales pan by PAN_ASPECT_RATIO(0.5) *
   amplitude * 0.45, but tilt by just amplitude * 0.45. Pan deviation
   is half of tilt deviation at the same amplitude. Was dividing both
   by 0.45, under-scaling pan by 2x. Now uses separate scales:
   - L2_AMP_SCALE_PAN  = 0.5 * 0.45 = 0.225
   - L2_AMP_SCALE_TILT = 0.45

2. ORBIT_RADIUS: Increased from 3.0m to 6.0m. At 3m, the angular
   change for a fixture 6m up was only ~8° at 50% amplitude. At 6m,
   it's ~16° at 50% and ~30° at 100% — visible and dramatic.

3. TILT → Y MAPPING: Was mapping tilt_base to Z (depth), which
   produces minimal angular change for ceiling fixtures. Now maps
   tilt_base to Y (height), so vertical sweep moves the target
   up/down → fixtures tilt up/down visibly.

At 50/50 (default): pan sweeps ±3m on X, tilt sweeps ±3m on Y.
At 100/100: pan sweeps ±6m, tilt sweeps ±6m — full stage coverage.
