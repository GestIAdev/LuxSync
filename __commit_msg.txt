WAVE 7627: Remove hard clamp, cap distScale at 1.0

The WAVE 7626 MAX_OFFSET_DMX hard clamp created a "chopped sine wave"
artifact — the top-center fixture hit the artificial software ceiling
of 100 DMX, paused while the underlying math exceeded it, then returned,
visually chopping off ~30° of the sweep arc.

Root cause: distScale (from _spatialDistanceScales) can reach 2.0 for
overhead fixtures, doubling the already-massive L2 pattern amplitude.
The distScale was designed for the VMM's gentle L0 offsets (±0.3), not
the L2 engine's full ±1.0 offsets.

Fix: Removed the MAX_OFFSET_DMX / MAX_OFFSET_DMX_16 hard clamps entirely.
Instead, cap the effective distScale to 1.0 (Math.min(rawDistScale, 1))
in both the 8-bit and 16-bit offset paths. This ensures distScale never
amplifies offsets — it only reduces them for far fixtures (its original
purpose). The L2 pattern amplitude is already user-controlled via the
amplitude slider, so distScale amplification is redundant.
