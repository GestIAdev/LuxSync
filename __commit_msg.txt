Each log line outputs:

This will reveal which variable is blowing up the delta and causing
the ~70% amplitude soft-clip on the top-center fixture.

HYPOTHESIS: The gimbalFactor fade is NOT the culprit (it only reduces
the delta, never amplifies). The likely culprit is that basePan is
already near a boundary (0 or 255) after the IK solve, leaving very
little headroom. Even a modest panDelta gets heavily compressed by
tanh when headroom is small. The fix would be to bias the IK solver
to keep the base pan away from the boundaries when L2 offsets are
active, or to increase the headroom by allowing slight overshoot.
