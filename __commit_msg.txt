WAVE 7618.3: Fix vertical sweep — map tilt to Z (depth) + increase radius to 8m

The sweep pattern (y=0, only pan oscillates) was producing a vertical
sweep because the L2 engine adds a constant orientation offset
(tiltOffsetNorm) to tilt_base for ceiling mounts. This constant offset
was being treated as pattern deviation and mapped to Y (height),
causing a visible vertical shift that looked like a vertical sweep.

Fix: Map tilt deviation to Z (depth) instead of Y (height).
- The constant orientation offset becomes a harmless depth shift
- Sweep (y=0): only pan deviation → pure horizontal X movement,
  matching classic sweep behavior
- Circle/bounce: tilt oscillation → Z (depth) movement, still visible
- ORBIT_RADIUS increased from 6.0m to 8.0m for more amplitude

At 50/50: pan sweeps ±4m on X, tilt shifts ~1.3m on Z (constant).
At 100/100: pan sweeps ±8m, full stage width coverage.
